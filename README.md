# Moonbeam

The official website for the **Moonbeam children’s book series by Hari Blossom**.

Follow Moonbeam, a very special dragon who breathes magical stardust, as she explores enchanted forests, mysterious mountains and magical lands, meeting extraordinary friends and discovering new adventures along the way.

The website brings together the Moonbeam books, characters and stories in one magical home, with information about each book and a growing collection of adventures from the world of Moonbeam.
