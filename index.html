About Moonbeam

Moonbeam is a growing collection of magical children’s stories created by Hari Blossom, following the adventures of a very special dragon who breathes sparkling stardust instead of fire.

At the heart of every story is Moonbeam’s kindness, curiosity and courage. Along the way she meets an ever-growing family of remarkable friends, including Blaze, Willow, Flashwing, Shard and Rainwing, each with their own personality, magical gifts and adventures to share.

The stories take Moonbeam from peaceful villages and enchanted forests to distant mountains, mysterious islands and magical lands. While every adventure is different, they are all built around simple themes that children can understand and enjoy, including friendship, helping others, courage, teamwork, kindness and celebrating what makes each of us different.

As the world of Moonbeam continues to grow, this website will grow with it. You’ll be able to discover each book, meet the characters, read story extracts, explore artwork and illustrations, and follow the latest news as new adventures are created.

There are many more places for Moonbeam to explore, many more friends to meet, and plenty more stardust still to come.

The Dragons Are Gathering

Moonbeam is no longer adventuring alone.

Blaze, Willow, Flashwing, Shard and Rainwing are coming together, each with their own personality and special abilities. Their adventures will take them far beyond Moonbeam's familiar skies and into mysterious new places where they will need friendship, courage and teamwork.

Exploring the Enchanted Forest

One of Moonbeam's coming adventures takes her deep into an Enchanted Forest filled with enormous trees, glowing leaves, strange riddles and ancient magic.

Something has gone terribly wrong in the forest, and Moonbeam and her friends must discover what is causing it before the darkness spreads any further.

Meet Midnight, the Shadow Dragon

A mysterious new dragon has appeared in Moonbeam's world.

Midnight has scales as dark as the night sky and the unusual ability to disappear into the shadows. Other dragons are frightened of him because he is different, but Moonbeam soon discovers that there is much more to Midnight than anyone realises.

His story is about friendship, understanding and finding where you belong.

Adventures Beyond the Dragon World

Moonbeam's adventures are beginning to take her further than ever before.

In one story she travels to the mysterious Echoing Mountains, where the mountains themselves seem to whisper and guide her.

In another, Moonbeam reaches the ocean and meets Aqua, a dolphin who desperately needs her help to protect the world beneath the waves.

And there are even stranger adventures ahead, including a mysterious old toy house where forgotten toys seem to have minds of their own.

More Moonbeam Magic Is Coming

Moonbeam's world is still growing, with new stories, new friends and magical places waiting to be discovered.

There are more adventures ahead, from mysterious forests and echoing mountains to shadowy dragons, enchanted houses and journeys beneath the ocean. Along the way, Moonbeam will meet new characters, face unexpected challenges and learn more about courage, kindness, friendship and helping others.

We'll also be sharing more from behind the scenes, including early artwork, character introductions, story extracts and a first look at future Moonbeam books.
